The retreat

A quiet village in the South of France.

Zomerstage takes you to a peaceful village near Mont Ventoux and Provençal towns such as Lourmarin and Bonnieux. For six nights, the entire resort is reserved for Zomerstage — just us, the dancing, and the landscape.

At its heart: a huge open-air pool, 25 by 10 metres, for the long golden afternoons between classes.

6Nights included
5Full class days
95+Class hours offered

How the days flow

Classes at the edges of the day. The middle is yours.

We dance and practise yoga in the cooler hours — mornings and late evenings — and leave the warm afternoons free. Wander into nearby towns, walk out into nature, go canoeing, visit the lavender museum, or simply stay by the pool.

Each evening brings practice or social dancing after dinner, with one shared theme night mid-week.

Mornings & evenings

What's included

Your stay, meals and classes.

Not included

Pricing

Your room sets the price.

Prices are per person and include six nights, all meals, and every class you choose. Transport and drinks are separate.

The Auberge's rooms are simple and full of character — some with exposed stone, a fireplace or a small terrace. Most have a private bathroom, and linens and towels are provided in every room except the sleeping loft.

Sleeping loft A spot in the shared loft (max. 15) — bring your own sheets and a towel. €670
4-person room You and three others you book with. €895
3-person room You and two others you book with. €950
2-person room You and one other you book with. €980
Single room A room to yourself. €1,095
How payment works
A €150 deposit per person secures your spot, due within 8 days of registration. A second instalment of €250 is due by 15 January 2027. The remaining balance is requested closer to the retreat. You'll receive an email with full payment instructions.
Cancellation policy
Cancel before 1 January 2027 → €250. Before 1 March 2027 → €500. After 1 March 2027 → the full amount is due. Fees are charged per participant.

Common questions

FAQ

When and where is Zomerstage 2027?

3–9 July 2027 — arrive Saturday 3 July, leave Friday 9 July, six nights in all. It's held in a peaceful village in the South of France, near Mont Ventoux and towns such as Lourmarin and Bonnieux, with the whole resort reserved for participants.

Who is it for — do I need experience?

The programme includes speed courses for first-time dancers and advanced courses for dancers with more experience.

What's included in the price?

Your six-night stay, three organic and locally sourced meals a day plus an afternoon snack, and all the dance and yoga classes you choose (95+ hours offered). All meals are vegetarian, with the option to eat non-vegetarian at dinner. Transport and drinks are not included.

What are the accommodation options and prices?

Per person, including your stay, meals and classes: sleeping loft €670, 4-person room €895, 3-person room €950, 2-person room €980, single room €1,095.

How do I register?

Registration opens in October 2026. Until then, subscribe to the newsletter and you'll be the first to know when spots open.

How does payment work?

A €150 deposit per person secures your spot, due within 8 days of registering. A second instalment of €250 follows by 15 January 2027, and the remaining balance is requested closer to the retreat. You'll receive an email with full payment instructions.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancel before 1 January 2027 and the fee is €250; before 1 March 2027 it's €500; after 1 March 2027 the full amount is due. Fees are charged per participant.
