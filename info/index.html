Arrival & departure

The dates and times below affect what train you should book — please read this before anything else.

  • We expect you at the Auberge on Saturday 3 July, from 17:00. Arriving earlier isn't possible — we need the time to change over groups and set up, so please don't arrive before 17:00.
  • Please don't book the night before at the Auberge des Seguins itself — staying nearby is of course fine, just not at the Auberge. You can always enjoy an ice cream on a terrace in a nearby village before you arrive.
  • The retreat ends on Friday 9 July, after breakfast. Breakfast starts at 8:00, which is also when reception opens to settle your drinks bill from the week — so don't book a return train that leaves too early (more under “Getting there”).
  • The final class timetable will be published on our website closer to the date.

Travel

Getting there

The Auberge is about 15 minutes from Apt and an hour from Avignon, tucked at the end of a valley.

By train (TGV)

Most participants travel by TGV. Routes via Lille are often practical; book early for the widest choice of fares.

Booking: thetrainline.com · ouigo.com

From Avignon TGV to Buoux

Take the bus from Avignon TGV station — e.g. Ligne 915 to Apt (search “bus Avignon gare TGV - Apt” for times). At the Gare Routière in Apt, book a taxi or Uber on the spot, ideally sharing with a few others. The ride to the Auberge takes about 15 minutes and costs €40–60.

Your return trip

Don't book your return TGV too early. Breakfast on Friday starts at 8:00 (when reception opens for drinks bills). A taxi from the Auberge to Apt takes ~15 min — but it'll be hard to find one before 8:00 — and the bus from Apt to Avignon TGV takes about 1.5 hours.

  1. From Bonnieux, take the D36 towards Lourmarin (about 5 km).
  2. Turn left onto the D943.
  3. After 2 km, turn right onto the D113.
  4. After 1.5 km, turn right again (just before Buoux) and follow a small road for another 3.5 km.

  6. The Auberge lies at the end of the valley. When the asphalt turns to an unpaved track it may feel wrong, but keep going slowly and follow your GPS.

Carpooling

Closer to the retreat we'll open a participant group for carpooling and questions — watch your inbox. As a guideline (4 people per car), we suggest €202 per person for the round trip (2064 km at €0.30/km + €186 tolls = €806, split by four). With fewer people per car, this rises accordingly.

Pack for it

What to bring

  • Dance shoes — several pairs (not your best). There are 3 floors: ballet floor, OSB and tiles.
  • A yoga mat — if you'd like to join the yoga classes (we don't provide them).
  • Swimwear and towels for the pool.
  • A water bottle to refill throughout the day.
  • Enough t-shirts, deodorant and shower gel for the full week.
  • Sunscreen, aftersun, sunglasses and a sun hat — classes are outdoors, in the sun.
  • Insect spray for mosquitoes and other insects.
  • Sheets or a sleeping bag and towels — only if you're in the sleeping loft. Linens and towels are provided in every other room.

On site

Your stay

A relaxed week, with classes for first-time and experienced dancers.

The setting

The Auberge sits in the Aiguebrun valley, in the heart of the Luberon, tucked at the foot of a tall cliff that shelters it from the Mistral — no traffic, no light pollution. Hiking trails start at the door, climbing spots are ten minutes away, and the huge open-air pool is at the centre of it all.

Your room

The rooms are simple and full of character — some with exposed rock, a fireplace or a small terrace. You'll be in the room type you booked, together with the people you booked with. Most rooms have a private bathroom; the sleeping loft has shared showers and toilets. Linens and towels are provided in every room except the loft.

Meals & drinks

Dinner seating — good to know

All meals at the retreat are vegetarian. The one choice is dinner: you could opt to eat non-vegetarian there, and non-vegetarian diners sit at their own table so the kitchen can serve everyone efficiently. You made that choice when you registered — so if you'd like to sit with friends or a partner at dinner, make sure you selected the same option.

Three organic, locally sourced meals a day, plus an afternoon snack. Water is provided at meals and during classes; other drinks can be bought on site and aren't included in the price.
